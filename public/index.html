Your Journey

How to Get Started with Forex

01

Learn the Basics

Understand currency pairs, pips, lots, leverage, and how the forex market operates globally.

02

Study Market Analysis

Master both technical analysis (charts, indicators) and fundamental analysis (economic data, news).

03

Practice on Demo

Use a free demo trading account to practice your strategies without risking real capital. Build confidence and test your system first.

04

Develop Your System

Create a consistent, rules-based trading system with clear entry, exit, and risk management rules.

Trust Fund Investment Club

Established in 2025, TFIC was founded with the vision of building a modern investment ecosystem focused on financial education, portfolio growth, and sustainable wealth creation in Tanzania and beyond.

Who We Are

Trust Fund Investment Club (TFIC) is a modern investment development company established to provide financial education, portfolio development, proprietary trading solutions, and diversified business investment opportunities.

The organization was founded with the objective of building a strong financial ecosystem that promotes wealth creation through professional investment systems, strategic risk management, and long-term business sustainability.

TFIC operates through multiple business divisions including financial education, proprietary trading operations, agribusiness, real estate development, and future institutional investment management services.

Our approach combines technology, financial knowledge, and diversified asset development to create sustainable economic opportunities for individuals, investors, and communities across Tanzania, Africa, and the world.

Our Mission

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 24, 2026 · Trust Fund Investment Club

1. Introduction

Trust Fund Investment Club ("TFIC", "we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or use our services. By using TFIC services, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, city/region, and account credentials provided during registration.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with our website, including pages visited, time spent, and features used.
  • Communication Data: Messages, inquiries, and correspondence sent through our contact form or email.
  • Newsletter Preferences: Email subscription status and communication preferences.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and manage your TFIC member account
  • Send market updates, financial insights, and educational content (if subscribed)
  • Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • Improve our platform, services, and user experience
  • Send important notices regarding your account or service changes
  • Comply with legal obligations and regulatory requirements

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information. Passwords are encrypted and stored securely. We use HTTPS encryption for all data transmission. Access to personal data is restricted to authorized personnel only. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information only in the following circumstances:

  • With service providers who assist in operating our platform (e.g., hosting, email delivery)
  • When required by law or regulatory authority
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of TFIC, our members, or others

6. Cookies & Tracking

Our website may use cookies to enhance your browsing experience, remember your preferences, and analyze website traffic. You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings, although this may affect some functionality of our platform.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and personal data
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time
  • Data portability — receive a copy of your data in a usable format

To exercise these rights, contact us at info@tfic.co.tz

8. Children's Privacy

TFIC services are not directed at individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us:
